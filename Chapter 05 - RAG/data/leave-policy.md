# Leave policy

## Time off

PTO means paid time off.
Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave.
Leave is counted in working days. Weekends do not use up a leave day.
Requests for more than 5 days in a row need a manager's approval two weeks ahead.
Unused leave can be carried into the next year, up to 5 days.

## Sick leave

Sick leave must be reported to the manager before 09:30.
A note from a doctor is required from the fourth sick day.
The studio pays the full salary for the first 30 sick days of the year.

## Parental leave

Parental leave lasts 16 weeks.
It can be shared between two parents who both work at the studio.
The employee tells HR at least 4 weeks before the leave starts.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely 3 days per week.
Tuesday is an office day for every team.
Remote days are not a substitute for annual leave.
The employee keeps the same working hours, 09:00 to 18:00, on a remote day.

## Year end

The studio closes from 24 December to 1 January.
Those closure days are not deducted from the 25 days of annual leave.
