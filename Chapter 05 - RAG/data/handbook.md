# Northwind Studio Handbook

## Company

Northwind Studio was founded in 2014 in Lyon by Amira Benali and Jonas Keller.
The studio builds software for teams that live in their documents.
Its main product family is called Lumen.
The legal name of the company is Northwind Studio SAS.
The head office is on Rue de la Republique in Lyon.
Amira leads product. Jonas leads the platform team.

## Products

Lumen is a documentation assistant sold as two products.

### Lumen Search

Lumen Search answers questions over a team's documents.
It costs 12 EUR per seat each month.
A seat is one named person. Shared logins are not allowed.
Search keeps a citation under every answer so a reader can open the source page.

### Lumen Draft

Lumen Draft writes the first version of a page from those documents.
It costs 18 EUR per seat each month.
Draft never publishes on its own. A human reviews the page before it goes live.
Teams use Draft for handbooks, release notes, and onboarding pages.

## Customers

Northwind Studio serves internal teams, not the general public.
A typical customer is a company with 50 to 500 employees.
The studio does not sell to schools or to hospitals.
New customers start on a 14 day trial of Lumen Search.
A trial does not include Lumen Draft.

## Support

Every support ticket starts with the code LMN-.
Example: LMN-1042 is the ticket for a failed login.
The support desk is open on weekdays from 08:00 to 19:00 Lyon time.
The on-call rotation has two shifts.
The first shift covers weekdays and is handled by the support desk.
The second shift covers weekends and is handled by the platform team.
Urgent tickets opened on a weekend are answered by the platform team within 2 hours.

## Service

The monthly uptime target is 99.5 percent.
Planned maintenance is announced 48 hours ahead and is not counted against that target.
Chat logs are kept for 30 days.
Uploaded documents are kept for 1 year.
A customer can ask for a document to be deleted sooner, and the studio does it within 7 days.

## Offices

The Lyon office is open from 09:00 to 18:00.
Visitors sign in at reception on the ground floor.
Meeting rooms are booked in the tool called Roomly.
The studio has no office in Paris. Staff travel there by train when a customer asks for an on-site visit.
