# Onboarding

## First week

Day 1 is security training.
The training covers passwords, badges, and how to report a lost laptop.
Day 2 is the product walkthrough of Lumen Search and Lumen Draft.
A buddy from the same team is assigned before the start date.
The buddy meets the new hire at reception at 09:15 on day 1.

## Equipment

New hires receive a Framework 13 laptop.
The laptop stays the property of Northwind Studio.
Accounts are created within 2 business days.
The employee id format is NW- followed by four digits, for example NW-0042.
The id is printed on the badge and used to log in to Roomly.

## Access

Off-site work requires the VPN client named Northwind Tunnel.
The VPN is required on any network that is not the Lyon office.
HR sends the VPN setup link on the Friday before the start date.
A personal phone cannot be used as the only way to receive the login code.
