# Expenses

## Travel

Train travel between Lyon and Paris is pre-approved.
Flights need a manager's approval before booking.
The daily meal allowance on a travel day is 35 EUR.
Taxis are allowed only when no train or metro reaches the customer site.

## Software

A tool under 20 EUR per month can be bought without approval.
Anything above that goes through the tools board.
The tools board meets every other Thursday.
Bought tools must be paid with the company card, not a personal card.
